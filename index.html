The Attention Toolkit

Five root causes of off-task behavior, five folders of strategies. Each one is a quick transition activity to move students from recess, PE, or the hallway into a focused, ready-to-learn state — before the reading or writing even starts.

Root cause 1

Cognitive Load

Students don't know what to do or how to do it. Strategies that strip away confusion so attention goes to the task, not the instructions.

 Root cause 2

Relevance

Students don't find the task meaningful or interesting. Strategies that hook curiosity and connect to what students already know.

 Root cause 3

Modality

Not enough variety in how students engage. Strategies that rotate through visual, auditory, and movement-based entry points.

 Root cause 4

Belonging

Students don't feel connected to the room. Strategies that build a felt sense of safety and relationship before asking for focus.

 Root cause 5

Autonomy

Students lack choice or a way to self-monitor. Strategies that hand back control and build the skill of noticing one's own focus.
Built on the five-root-cause framework from Robin Wisniewski's Promoting Student Attention.
